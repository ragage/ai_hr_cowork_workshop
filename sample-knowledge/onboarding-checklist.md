# New-Hire Onboarding Checklist (Sample)

> **SAMPLE / FICTIONAL CONTENT** for Copilot Cowork training.

## Before Day 1 (HR + Manager)
- [ ] Send welcome email with start date, first-day logistics, and dress code.
- [ ] Create accounts (email, HR portal, collaboration tools) via IT ticket.
- [ ] Ship laptop and access badge; confirm home-office stipend if remote.
- [ ] Assign an onboarding buddy.
- [ ] Prepare a 30-day plan with the hiring manager.

## Day 1
- [ ] Welcome meeting with manager and buddy.
- [ ] Complete HR paperwork (tax forms, direct deposit, emergency contacts).
- [ ] Review the employee handbook and code of conduct.
- [ ] Set up workstation, email signature, and calendar.
- [ ] Enroll in benefits (reminder: within 30 days of start).

## Week 1
- [ ] Meet the immediate team; schedule 1:1s with key partners.
- [ ] Complete required compliance training (security, harassment prevention).
- [ ] Review the 30-day plan and first goals with manager.
- [ ] Book recurring 1:1 with manager.

## Days 30 / 60 / 90
- [ ] **Day 30:** Check-in on ramp-up, questions, and access issues.
- [ ] **Day 60:** Review progress against the 30-day plan; adjust goals.
- [ ] **Day 90:** Formal onboarding review; confirm role expectations and development goals.

## Owner Reference
| Task area | Owner |
| --- | --- |
| Accounts & equipment | IT |
| Paperwork & benefits | HR Service Desk |
| Role ramp-up & goals | Hiring manager |
| Day-to-day questions | Onboarding buddy |
