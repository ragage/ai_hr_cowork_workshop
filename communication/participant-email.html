What you'll do

Eight hands-on exercises built around everyday HR work:

  • Build an executive command center from your mail, calendar, and chats
  • Research the web with citations, and have Cowork browse websites for you
  • Build your own custom skill that answers HR policy questions
  • Draft a job posting, put together an onboarding pack, and schedule recurring work
  • Finish by building a no-code HR Policy Agent with Copilot Agent Builder

Good to know

  • The first exercise builds a private dashboard from your own mail, calendar, and Teams. Only you see the results, and nothing is shown on screen. Every other exercise uses fictional sample files, so please don't bring or upload real employee data.
  • Everything Cowork creates is a draft for you to review. It asks before it sends or shares anything.
  • You work in your own account, so your OneDrive, drafts, and skills are your own.
