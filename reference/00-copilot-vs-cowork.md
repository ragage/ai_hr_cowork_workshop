# 00 · Copilot vs. Cowork — What's the Difference?

> Reference sheet for the "Getting Things Done with Copilot Cowork for HR Tasks" workshop.
> This is where the training **starts** — get the mental model right before touching the product.

## The one-sentence difference

- **Microsoft 365 Copilot (Chat)** is your **AI assistant** — you ask, it answers, and you stay in
  control of each step.
- **Copilot Cowork** is your **AI coworker** — you describe an **outcome**, and it plans the steps,
  picks the skills, does the multi-step work, and hands back a finished artifact for your review.

Think: **Copilot helps you do the work. Cowork does the work for you** (with your approval at the
key moments).

## Side-by-side

| | **Copilot (Chat)** | **Copilot Cowork** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Quick answers, drafting, summarizing, rewriting | Multi-step tasks that end in a deliverable |
| Interaction | Turn-by-turn conversation | Describe the outcome; it runs the plan |
| Who drives | **You**, step by step | **Cowork** plans & executes; you approve checkpoints |
| Output | A response in the chat | Finished artifacts: doc, deck, email, report, schedule |
| Runs across | Your M365 apps as an assistant | Skills + apps orchestrated for you (Work IQ) |
| Long-running / scheduled | No | Yes — **Automations** run on a schedule or on events |
| Example (HR) | "Summarize this policy in 3 bullets" | "Draft welcome emails for all 4 new hires, ground them in our onboarding checklist, and save them as drafts" |

## When to use which (HR examples)

**Reach for Copilot (Chat) when…**
- You want a fast answer: *"What's our PTO carryover rule?"*
- You're polishing text you already have: *"Make this message warmer."*
- You need a quick summary of one document or thread.

**Reach for Cowork when…**
- The task has **several steps** and ends in a **deliverable**: *"Build an onboarding orientation
  deck from our checklist and benefits summary."*
- You want it to **act** across apps: draft + schedule + notify.
- You want it to **repeat on a schedule**: *"Every Monday, summarize open HR tickets."*

> **Rule of thumb:** If you'd normally open several apps and it ends in a file, email, schedule, or
> report — that's a **Cowork** job. If you just need an answer or a quick rewrite — that's **Copilot
> Chat**.

## How they relate

Cowork isn't a *replacement* for Copilot Chat — it's the **"get it done for me"** mode of the same
Microsoft 365 Copilot family. Both are grounded in your work through **Work IQ** and respect the same
permissions. You'll switch between them: Chat for quick help, Cowork for finished work.

## What this means for today

- We spend the workshop in **Cowork** because HR work is full of multi-step, deliverable-producing
  tasks.
- At the very end we step **outside Cowork** to build a **persistent agent** with **Copilot Agent
  Builder** (Exercise 8) — a different tool for when you want a **reusable, shareable Q&A agent**
  rather than a one-off task. See [08-agent-builder-policy-agent.md](08-agent-builder-policy-agent.md).
