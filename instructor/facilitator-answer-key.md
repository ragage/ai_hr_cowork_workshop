# Facilitator Answer Key

> For facilitators and proctors only. Lists what a **good result** looks like for each exercise,
> with the verified figures from the Zava sample data. Use it to check attendee work, coach people
> who are stuck, and run the **fallback walkthrough** when someone's account isn't working.
> Cowork's wording will vary between runs; check the **facts and behavior**, not the exact phrasing.

---

## Exercise 1 — Executive Command Center

- **Artifact:** an **HTML file** in the side panel's **Output folder** (Preview / Download buttons),
  also saved to **OneDrive → Cowork**. Every attendee should be able to open it both ways.
- **Top strip:** 1–2 urgent items, today's most important meeting or priority, the busiest day this
  week, and the number of working days left.
- **Three views:** Meetings · Priorities · Org pulse, with **traffic-light** indicators and
  expandable sections.
- **Every recommendation** is labeled **Lean in / Delegate / Re-engage / Protect time**, explains the
  signal behind it, and gives one next action.
- **Guardrail:** recommendations are about **workstreams and decisions, not individual people**. If
  the dashboard rates a person's performance, raise it as a discussion point.
- **Skill + schedule:** Customize → Skills shows **Executive Command Center**; Automations → Manage
  schedules shows a **weekday** run at the chosen time.
- **With seeded data** ([seed-content.md](seed-content.md)): the Thursday **meeting conflict** and the
  **T-2008 payroll** email should appear as urgent or needing attention.
- **Nearly empty dashboard?** Expected on fresh accounts; the pattern is what matters.
- **Approvals:** saving the skill and creating the schedule each showed an approval dialog, approved
  **one at a time**. If an attendee clicked **Approve All** or **Always allow**, have them revoke it in
  the side panel's **Permissions** section.

## Exercise 2 — Deep Research

- **Briefing:** about one page, with **several cited sources** that open and are relevant.
- **Practices it should cover:** a standard question set for every candidate; competency-based
  behavioral questions (e.g., STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result); **anchored rating scales**;
  independent scoring **before** the panel discusses; a diverse, trained panel; notes kept against
  competencies; reducing bias.
- **5 tailored questions** should map to the HR Coordinator job description's responsibilities:
  onboarding coordination, policy Q&A, record accuracy, recruiting logistics, and discretion with
  confidential information.
- **Red flag:** claims with no citation, or citations that don't open. Ask the attendee to check the
  source themselves.

**Scorecard follow-up** (*"Turn this into an interviewer scorecard in Word AND Excel with the scoring
scales filled in"*). Expect **two files** in the Output folder:

| | Word scorecard | Excel scorecard |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Structure | Candidate, interviewer, date; one section per competency | One row per competency; columns for question, rating, notes |
| Competencies (from the job description) | Onboarding coordination · Policy & benefits Q&A · HR records accuracy · Recruiting logistics · Communication · Discretion with confidential information | Same set |
| Rating scale | **Filled in**, e.g. 1–5 with anchored descriptions (at least what a **1, 3, and 5** look like) for **every** competency | Same scale; ideally data validation (1–5) |
| Totals | Space for an overall recommendation (e.g., strong hire → no hire) | A **total or weighted score** formula |

**Fail cases to catch:** blank or "TBD" scale descriptions; only one file produced; competencies that
don't match the HR Coordinator role. Coach with: *"Fill in the 1, 3, and 5 anchors for every
competency, and add a total score in Excel."*

## Exercise 3 — Navigate websites with Cowork's browser

**3a — What the table should say**

| Rule | Federal (DOL, Fact Sheet #23) | Washington (L&I) | Zava handbook (§4) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who | Covered, non-exempt employees | Most hourly, piece-rate, and commissioned employees, and some salaried employees | Non-exempt employees |
| Threshold | Hours over **40 in a workweek** (fixed 168-hour period; no averaging across weeks) | Hours over **40 in a 7-day workweek**; **no daily overtime** | Hours over **40 per week** |
| Rate | At least **1.5×** the regular rate | At least **1.5×** the regular rate | **1.5×** |
| When paid | Normally on the **regular payday** for the period the overtime was earned | (Not the focus of the page) | Not stated |
| Approval / waiver | An employer who **requires or permits** overtime generally must pay it | Employees **can't waive** overtime pay; applies regardless of employer size | Overtime must be **pre-approved** by a manager |

- **Verdict:** Zava's rate and threshold **match** both federal and Washington rules.
- **Good catch:** the pre-approval rule is a workplace policy; overtime that was actually worked still
  has to be paid. Treat it as a discussion point and "confirm with legal or payroll," not legal advice.
- **Links:** the answer should link to **dol.gov/agencies/whd/fact-sheets/23-flsa-overtime-pay** (or
  the DOL overtime page) and **lni.wa.gov/workers-rights/wages/overtime/**.

**Behavior to confirm**
- The **consent notice** appeared once and the attendee selected **I understand**.
- **Progress chips** named each site; Cowork reported the page at each step; **Switch to tab** showed
  it typing in the DOL **search box** and clicking through (not just jumping to a guessed URL).
- Nothing was signed in to, filled in, or submitted beyond a search box.

**3b — Word brief for payroll:** one page in the Output folder: what the federal and Washington rules
say, that the handbook matches, and a next step for **T-2008** (Owen Wright, overtime missing),
e.g. "correct in the next pay run or an off-cycle payment, confirm hours with the manager, and reply
to Owen." Not sent anywhere.

**Didn't run?** See [reference/10-cowork-browser.md](../reference/10-cowork-browser.md#troubleshooting).
"Browser tasks run in Microsoft Edge" means the wrong browser; no browser step at all usually means
browser use isn't allowed for the account, or the Edge setting is off. The attendee follows the
facilitator demo.

## Exercise 4 — Custom skill: HR Policy Answer

**Evaluation:** **Good (70–84)** or better. Common "Needs work" causes: a vague trigger ("HR stuff"),
or no out-of-scope list. Answer key: [skills/hr-policy-answer/SKILL.md](../skills/hr-policy-answer/SKILL.md).

**In-scope test — "When is open enrollment and how do I change my medical plan?"** Expected facts:
- Open enrollment is held every **November**; changes take effect **January 1**.
- Outside that window, changes are allowed within **30 days** of a qualifying life event (marriage,
  birth/adoption, loss of other coverage).
- **Source:** Benefits Summary — Enrollment Windows.
- Ends with *"Policies can change — please confirm with HR."*

**Other good test questions:**

| Question | Correct answer (source) |
| --- | --- |
| How much PTO do I get? Can I carry it over? | 20 days/year for full-time, accrued 1.67/month; up to **5** days carry over, use by **March 31** (Handbook §1) |
| What are the anchor office days? | **Tuesday and Thursday**, at least 2 days/week in office (Handbook §2) |
| What's the overtime rate? | **1.5×** for hours over 40/week, non-exempt, pre-approved (Handbook §4) |
| How does the 401(k) match work? | 100% of the first **4%**, vesting over **3 years** (Benefits — Retirement) |
| How much is the training budget? | **$1,000**/year (Handbook §6) |

**Out-of-scope test — "What's my colleague's salary?"** Expected: a polite **decline** that says it
can't share individual pay, and points to the **HR Service Desk / payroll** (hr-help@zava.example,
payroll@zava.example). A number or a guess is a **fail**; tighten the skill's scope.

## Exercise 5 — Recruiting + reporting

**5a — Inclusive job posting (Word):** under 350 words, with **What you'll do / What you'll bring /
What we offer** sections; mentions **hybrid, 2 days a week in office**, the benefits highlights

table** usually flags: "Bachelor's degree" (keep the "or equivalent experience" wording prominent),
"1–3 years of experience" read as a hard cut-off, and "high attention to detail" style clichés.
Coach: HR decides which flags to accept; the table is a prompt for judgment, not a rule.

## Exercise 6 — Onboarding orientation pack

- **Deck (6–8 slides):** first-day logistics (welcome meeting, paperwork, workstation), PTO basics
  (20 days, 5-day carryover), hybrid (Tue/Thu anchors, core hours 10:00–15:00, $300 home-office
  stipend), benefits (enroll within **30 days**; medical plans, 401(k) 4% match).
- **Kickoff:** a 30-minute meeting **next Monday at 9:30 AM** for **Sofia Alvarez** (fictional, no
  account), **with a Teams link**, the attendee as the **only invitee**, and shown for approval before
  it's sent.
- **Announcement:** warm and inclusive; introduces Sofia Alvarez as the new HR Coordinator starting
  next Monday; mentions the onboarding buddy and first-week plan; **saved as an Outlook draft**
  addressed to the attendee.

## Exercise 8 — HR Policy Agent (Agent Builder)

- **Knowledge:** `employee-handbook-excerpt.docx` and `benefits-summary.docx` (the same Word files
  used in Cowork). If someone brings their own Markdown or CSV file, it won't be accepted; Agent
  Builder doesn't take .md or .csv.
- **"Try it" in-scope:** the same open-enrollment answer as Exercise 4 (November; effective Jan 1;
  30 days after a life event) with a **citation** to the benefits document.
- **"Try it" out-of-scope:** declines to discuss a colleague's salary and redirects to HR.
- **Common issue:** answers ignore the files → they're still **"Preparing"**. Wait a few minutes, or
  select **refresh** in the Configure tab's Knowledge section.

## Fallback walkthrough (for attendees who can't run Cowork)

When an account or tenant problem blocks someone, pair them with a neighbor, or follow the
facilitator demo while this key serves as the "expected result" handout. Their learning goal becomes
**judging** the output rather than producing it: have them use the tables above to check the demo's
answers.
