# Zava — Employee Handbook (Excerpt)

> **SAMPLE / FICTIONAL CONTENT.** Zava is a fictional company. All names, policies,
> and figures below are invented for training with Copilot Cowork. Do not treat as real HR guidance.

## 1. Paid Time Off (PTO)

- **Annual allowance:** 20 days of PTO per calendar year for full-time employees, accrued monthly
  (1.67 days/month).
- **Carryover:** Up to 5 unused days may carry into the next year. They must be used by March 31 or
  they are forfeited.
- **Requesting PTO:** Submit requests in the HR portal at least **10 business days** in advance for
  absences longer than 3 days. Manager approval is required.
- **Sick leave:** Separate from PTO. 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for
  absences of 3 or more consecutive days.

## 2. Remote & Hybrid Work

- **Standard hybrid:** Employees work in the office **at least 2 days per week** (Tuesday and
  Thursday are anchor days for most teams).
- **Fully remote:** Requires VP approval and a signed remote-work agreement.
- **Equipment:** Zava provides a laptop and a one-time **$300 home-office stipend**.
- **Core hours:** 10:00–15:00 local time; employees should be reachable during these hours.

## 3. Code of Conduct (Summary)

- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect and professionalism.
- No harassment, discrimination, or retaliation of any kind. Report concerns to HR or via the
  anonymous ethics line.
- Protect confidential and customer information. Do not share data outside approved systems.
- Avoid conflicts of interest; disclose any outside employment or financial interest that could
  conflict with your role.

## 4. Working Hours & Overtime

- **Standard week:** 40 hours, Monday–Friday.
- **Overtime (non-exempt employees):** Paid at 1.5x for hours over 40 per week, pre-approved by a
  manager.
- **Flexible schedules:** Available with manager approval as long as core hours are covered.

## 5. Performance & Reviews

- **Cadence:** Formal reviews twice per year (mid-year check-in in June, annual review in December).
- **Ratings:** Exceeds / Meets / Developing.
- **Growth:** Every employee sets 3–5 goals per cycle aligned to team objectives.

## 6. Learning & Development

- **Training budget:** $1,000 per employee per year for approved courses, conferences, or
  certifications.
- **Internal mobility:** Employees may apply to open internal roles after 12 months in their
  current position.
