# Open Role: HR Coordinator (Sample Job Description)

> **SAMPLE / FICTIONAL CONTENT** for Copilot Cowork training.

**Company:** Zava
**Team:** People & Culture
**Location:** Hybrid (2 days/week in office)
**Reports to:** HR Manager

## About the Role
The HR Coordinator supports day-to-day People operations across the employee lifecycle — from
onboarding new hires to answering policy questions and keeping HR records accurate. This is a great
role for someone organized, people-focused, and eager to grow in HR.

## Responsibilities
- Coordinate new-hire onboarding: paperwork, accounts, first-week scheduling.
- Serve as first point of contact for employee questions on PTO, benefits, and policies.
- Maintain accurate HR records and generate simple reports.
- Support recruiting logistics: scheduling interviews and communicating with candidates.
- Help organize employee events and internal communications.

## Required Qualifications
- 1–3 years of experience in HR, administration, or a coordination role.
- Strong written and verbal communication.
- Comfortable with Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Word, Excel, Teams).
- High attention to detail and discretion with confidential information.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with an HRIS or HR service-desk tool.
- Familiarity with benefits enrollment processes.
- Bachelor's degree in HR, business, or a related field (or equivalent experience).

## What We Offer
- Competitive salary and annual bonus.
- Comprehensive benefits (medical, dental, vision, 401(k) match).
- $1,000 annual learning budget and internal mobility after 12 months.
