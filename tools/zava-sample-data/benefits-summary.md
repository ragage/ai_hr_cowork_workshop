# Zava — Benefits Summary (Sample)

> **SAMPLE / FICTIONAL CONTENT** for Copilot Cowork training. Not real benefits information.

## Health & Insurance

| Benefit | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Medical | Two plans: **Standard PPO** and **High-Deductible (HDHP + HSA)**. Zava covers 80% of premiums. |
| Dental | Covered 100% for preventive care; 50% for major work. |
| Vision | Annual eye exam plus $150 toward frames/lenses. |
| Life insurance | 2x annual salary, employer-paid. Additional coverage available. |
| Disability | Short-term and long-term disability included. |

## Retirement

- **401(k) match:** Zava matches 100% of the first 4% of salary you contribute.
- **Vesting:** Employer match vests over 3 years (33% per year).

## Enrollment Windows

- **New hires:** Must enroll within **30 days** of start date.
- **Open enrollment:** Held every year in **November**; changes take effect January 1.
- **Qualifying life events** (marriage, birth/adoption, loss of other coverage) allow changes
  within **30 days** of the event.

## Wellbeing & Extras

- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP):** Free confidential counseling, 8 sessions per issue per year.
- **Wellness stipend:** $50/month toward gym, fitness apps, or wellness activities.
- **Parental leave:** 12 weeks paid for the primary caregiver; 6 weeks for the secondary caregiver.
- **Commuter benefits:** Pre-tax transit and parking accounts available.

## Frequently Asked

- *"When can I change my plan?"* — During November open enrollment, or within 30 days of a
  qualifying life event.
- *"Does the HSA money roll over?"* — Yes, HSA balances roll over year to year and are yours to keep.
- *"How do I add a dependent?"* — Submit the change in the HR portal within 30 days of the life event.
